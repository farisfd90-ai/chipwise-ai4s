# Data, rules, and evidence provenance

ChipWise separates **competition rules**, **public scientific evidence**, and **synthetic quantitative demo data** so that none of them are confused with each other.

## Competition source

- 5th Pazhou Algorithm Competition — AI + Organ-on-a-Chip Open-ended Innovation Challenge: https://www.aicompetition-pz.com/topic_detail/26

The official challenge description permits public, literature, simulated, and synthetic data when sources, usage/licensing, assumptions, and limitations are documented. It also permits AI-assisted development when tools and usage are disclosed.

## Important distinction

`data/anchor_studies.csv` is a provenance/evidence catalog. It is **not** the quantitative training dataset.

`data/synthetic_liver_chip.csv` is generated by `src/simulate.py` from a disclosed deterministic simulator and fixed random seed. It contains **no copied experimental measurements** from the cited GEO studies.

The reported prediction and active-learning metrics therefore demonstrate software/model behavior on a controlled synthetic benchmark only. They must not be interpreted as biological validation, clinical accuracy, or wet-lab performance.

## Data-use note

This repository links to public metadata pages instead of redistributing restricted or third-party raw datasets. Anyone extending ChipWise with real experimental data is responsible for checking the relevant dataset, publication, institutional, consent, and licensing terms before reuse.
