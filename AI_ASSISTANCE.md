# AI assistance disclosure

The competition explicitly permits AI tools, including large language models and AI agents, when their sources and use are clearly disclosed.

## Tools used

- **OpenAI ChatGPT** — assisted with project ideation, public-source research, architecture discussion, Python implementation, debugging, testing strategy, documentation drafting, competition-rule interpretation, and review of scientific-claim wording.

## What AI did not provide

- No private competition data were accessed.
- No hidden answer key or evaluation labels were used.
- No patient-level or sensitive clinical data were supplied to the project.
- The runnable model does not call ChatGPT, an OpenAI API, or any proprietary LLM at runtime.
- Evaluation numbers in the repository are produced by the checked-in Python scripts, not invented model outputs.

## Human responsibility

The entrant remains responsible for the submitted project, licensing, factual accuracy, interpretation, and compliance. Synthetic-data and biological-validity limitations are explicitly documented so AI assistance is not used to inflate scientific claims.
